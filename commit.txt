Sound level meter PWA, built with Bun

A phone decibel meter that installs as a PWA and works offline. It is
deployed to GitHub Pages at https://nojaf.com/decibels/

- An AudioWorklet A-weights every microphone sample (three biquads,
  within IEC 61672 class 1 tolerance up to 12.5 kHz), keeps the Fast and
  Slow averages, and posts a frame every 50 ms.
- The session tracks Leq, min, max, peak and a 60 s history for dBA and
  dBZ, Fast and Slow. Levels are stored in dBFS and the calibration
  offset is added only when read.
- The calibration sheet shows whether the browser honoured turning off
  auto gain, noise suppression and echo cancellation.
- A screen wake lock keeps the meter running while it measures.
- Bun is the whole toolchain: its HTML bundler, Bun.serve for the dev and
  preview servers, and Bun.WebView for a browser test that feeds a known
  tone through Chrome's fake microphone.
- The service worker is hand-written and precaches every file in dist/.
- The icons and the palette come from Loudred (#294).
